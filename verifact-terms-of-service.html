Verifact Is a Neutral Platform

Verifact does not take sides.

We do not endorse, promote, or suppress any political party, ideology, religion, nationality, or viewpoint. Our platform exists solely to help communities evaluate the accuracy of claims — not to influence what people believe.

Verifact does not intervene in community verdicts. Once a claim enters the voting process, the outcome is determined entirely by community participation and the transparent verification algorithm. No Verifact employee, administrator, or AI system will alter, override, or manipulate a community verdict except in cases of confirmed fraud, illegal content, or court order.

The truth belongs to the community. Our job is to protect the process — not to decide the outcome.

Equal treatment guarantee: Verifact applies the same community rules, the same algorithm, and the same moderation standards to all claims regardless of the political, ideological, cultural, or geographic viewpoint of the subject matter. A claim about a left-leaning figure is evaluated by the same process as a claim about a right-leaning figure. No exceptions.

4.1 No Editorial Intervention

MD Media LLC and its employees do not:

  • Select which claims receive AI analysis or community attention
  • Adjust vote weights to favor a particular verdict
  • Delay or accelerate the verification timeline based on claim subject matter
  • Remove or suppress claims because of their political or ideological content
  • Instruct the AI system to produce a specific verdict on any claim
  • Interfere with the reputation system to elevate or suppress specific users

The only grounds for claim removal are violation of Community Rules (Section 4), prohibited conduct (Section 11), applicable law, or a valid court order. Disagreement with a verdict is not grounds for removal.

4.2 Algorithmic Transparency

Verifact publishes the core logic of its verification algorithm so that users and the public can understand how verdicts are reached. The weighting formula, phase timing, minimum vote thresholds, and trust score system are documented and accessible.

Final score formula:

Final verdict = (AI confidence × 40%) + (weighted community score × 60%)

Community voting always carries more weight than AI analysis. No single user, regardless of trust level or verification status, controls more than a capped vote weight in any verdict.

Verifact will notify users of any material changes to the verification algorithm at least 14 days before they take effect.

The Voting and Verification System

6.1 How Verdicts Work

Verifact uses a combination of community voting and AI analysis to produce verdicts on claims. The final verdict is determined by a weighted algorithm that combines AI confidence scores and community vote totals. No single vote, including that of verified users, administrators, or high-reputation contributors, determines a final verdict alone.

6.2 Verdict Accuracy

Verifact verdicts represent the collective judgment of the community at a point in time. They are not legal determinations of truth or falsity. Verdicts may change if new evidence is submitted or if claims are re-evaluated. Verifact makes no guarantee that any verdict is accurate, complete, or final.

6.3 One Vote Per User

Each registered user may cast one vote per claim. Attempting to cast multiple votes on the same claim through any method — including multiple accounts, automation, or exploiting platform bugs — is prohibited.

6.4 Vote Manipulation

Organizing, incentivizing, or coordinating votes to influence a verdict in a predetermined direction is a serious violation of these Terms and will result in account termination and potential legal action.

Verifact reserves the right to exclude votes detected as fraudulent, coordinated, or bot-generated from verdict calculations, with or without notice to the voter.
