AI Disclaimer

Verifact AI pre-checks are preliminary informational signals. AI can be incomplete, outdated, biased, unavailable, or wrong. AI does not decide final truth.

What AI May Review

Verifact may use claim text, source URLs, source-page text, and evidence context to estimate claim type, source quality, source support, confidence, and risk signals.

How To Use AI Results

  • Treat AI output as a starting point for review, not a final verdict.
  • Read the original source whenever possible.
  • Use community votes and evidence as additional context.
  • Report harmful, abusive, explicit, spammy, or manipulative content.

No Advice

Verifact and its AI signals are not legal, medical, financial, election, or safety advice. Always verify important information independently.
