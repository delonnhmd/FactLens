Community Guidelines

Verifact works only if users post claims and evidence in good faith. These rules apply to claims, evidence, images, reports, votes, usernames, profile text, and any other user-submitted content.

Allowed Use

  • Post claims that can be discussed with sources or evidence.
  • Add evidence that helps support, challenge, or add context to a claim.
  • Vote honestly and avoid coordinated manipulation.
  • Report unsafe, abusive, explicit, spammy, or misleading content.

Not Allowed

  • Nude, pornographic, sexually explicit, or exploitative content.
  • Hate, harassment, threats, abuse, or extremist promotion.
  • Spam, scams, malware, malicious links, impersonation, or coordinated manipulation.
  • Private personal data posted without permission.
  • Evidence or claim text designed to mislead users or evade moderation.

Moderation

Content may be reported, reviewed, hidden, removed, or restricted. Repeat abuse can lead to account action.
