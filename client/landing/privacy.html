Privacy Policy

Effective June 8, 2026

Verifact is a community-powered claim verification app. This policy explains how we collect and use data needed to operate accounts, claims, evidence, voting, reporting, moderation, and AI-assisted source checks.

Information We Collect

  • Account details such as email address, username, profile display name, avatar, and account status.
  • User-generated content such as claims, source URLs, evidence notes, uploaded images, votes, and reports.
  • Reputation and safety signals such as vote history, evidence counts, badges, report status, and moderation status.
  • Technical data needed to keep the app reliable, such as authentication session data and service logs.

How We Use Information

  • To create accounts, sign users in, and maintain profile and reputation records.
  • To publish claims, evidence, votes, reports, and public contributor cards inside Verifact.
  • To run AI pre-checks and source-support signals for informational review.
  • To investigate abuse, hide unsafe content, enforce community guidelines, and prevent spam.
  • To respond to support requests and operate the service.

Public Content

Claims, evidence, votes, public profile information, and reputation signals may be visible to other users. Do not submit private personal information in public claims or evidence.

AI Processing

Verifact may send claim text, source URLs, and related evidence context to AI systems for preliminary source and claim-risk review. AI output is informational and may be incorrect.

Account Deletion

You can request account deletion from Profile settings in the app. To preserve verification history, public claims, votes, and evidence may remain while your public account identity is anonymized.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, email support@factfight.com.
